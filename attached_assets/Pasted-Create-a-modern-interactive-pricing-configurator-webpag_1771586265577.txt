Create a modern interactive pricing configurator webpage for a SaaS product called "KIO".

Goal:
The visitor should understand the price logically by making business decisions.
No technical language like tokens, GPU, or context window.
Always explain price in plain business language.

Tech:
Single page app
Pure HTML, CSS and Vanilla JavaScript
No frameworks
Responsive layout
Clean enterprise design (white background, lots of whitespace, subtle grey borders, accent color #ff6a00)

Layout:
Left side = configuration panel
Right side = live summary + price explanation

Top headline:
"Configure how KIO should work in your organization"
Subheadline:
"The price depends on how KIO supports your daily work."

--------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 – Employees
--------------------------------------------------
Title: "How many people will use KIO?"

Slider:
Range: 1 – 500
Default: 10

Display below slider:
"10 employees work with KIO"

Price logic:
Base price per user = 49 €

--------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 – Working Mode
--------------------------------------------------
Title: "How intensively should KIO work?"

Two selectable cards (radio buttons):

Card 1:
Title: "Daily Assistance"
Description: "KIO answers questions and supports daily tasks"
Price: 49 € per user

Card 2:
Title: "Knowledge Work"
Description: "KIO analyzes extensive documents and prepares results"
Price: 119 € per user

Switching changes price per user.

--------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------
Title: "How much company knowledge should KIO know?"

Slider with steps:
100 MB
1 GB

200 GB
500 GB
1 TB

Price mapping:
100 MB = 0 €
1 GB = 9 €
10 GB = 29 €
50 GB = 79 €
200 GB = 149 €

Display dynamic explanation text depending on selection:
small → "individual documents"

large → "organizational memory"

--------------------------------------------------
Title: "Additional knowledge sources"

Monthly total =
