## Replit Prompt — KIO Homepage Hero Animation (Cable-Based Data Flow)

Build a fullscreen animated hero background for the KIO homepage.

This is NOT a particle animation.
It must look like a structured enterprise data infrastructure diagram where all systems are physically connected using visible data lines (cables).

The goal: immediately communicate that KIO is infrastructure orchestration, not a chatbot.

---

### General Style

* Dark background: `#0b1d2c`
* Premium enterprise consulting aesthetic
* Calm, slow animation
* No flickering, no randomness
* Everything deterministic and readable
* Smooth 60fps
* Seamless 10s loop
* Responsive scaling
* Reduce animation complexity on mobile

Use **Three.js** (preferred) with Canvas fallback.

---

### Central Element (Bottom Center)

Create a glass-like cube or sphere labeled:

KIO

Appearance:

* subtle glow `#ff6a1a`
* soft breathing pulse
* internal rotating rings or routing paths (slow motion)
* cables must visibly plug into the object (no floating connections)

This object represents the orchestration core.

---

### Left Side — Internal Systems (vertical descending stack)

Nodes:
ERP
CRM
Miro
Outlook
SharePoint
Teams
Atlassian
Database

Style:

* rectangular nodes
* blue color `#4da3ff`
* thin curved cable from each node to KIO
* cables always visible

Animation:

* small light pulses travel through the cable toward KIO every 1–2 seconds
* equal speed across all internal systems
  Meaning: operational company data

---

### Right Side — External Intelligence Sources

Nodes:
North Data
Statista

Dun & Bradstreet

Style:

* same geometry but violet `#9b7bff`
* cables slightly thicker
* slower pulse movement
* sometimes two signals merge into one before entering KIO

Meaning: external intelligence & signals

---

### Processing Visualization

When pulses reach KIO:

1. signal enters core
2. inside routing circle distributes signals
3. a single clean white line exits upward
4. fades near headline area

Color of decision line: white with soft glow

---

### Cable Behavior

* Use bezier curves
* Lines must stay visible permanently
* Pulses move inside the cable (like electricity in fiber)
* No floating particles anywhere

---

### Performance

* Pause heavy animation when tab inactive
* Reduce node count on small screens
* Maintain visual readability at all sizes
